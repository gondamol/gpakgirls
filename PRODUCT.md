# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective donors, institutional/philanthropic partners, volunteers, and the general public researching GPAK before giving, partnering, or referring a girl into the program. Many arrive skeptical of NGO claims and are actively comparing GPAK against other organizations competing for the same donor dollars. Secondary audience: the adolescent mothers and families GPAK serves, and community members who identify girls needing support.

## Product Purpose

Raise awareness of teenage pregnancy and HIV vulnerability among adolescent girls in western Kenya, showcase GPAK's programs and measurable impact, and convert visitors into donors, monthly givers, volunteers, or partners. Success looks like a visitor leaving convinced GPAK's model works and its numbers are real, then acting on it (donating, signing up, referring a partner).

## Brand Personality

Professional, credible, data-driven. The site should read like a well-run organization a due-diligence-minded funder would trust with their money, not a plea for sympathy. Evidence and mechanism (how the model works, what it measures) do the persuading, not emotional appeal alone.

## Anti-references

Not the generic charity-stock-photo site: no "sad African child" imagery, no poverty-porn framing, no treating the girls served as passive victims. Center dignity and agency. Avoid unverifiable "impact" claims stated as fact without a stated source, consistent with the site's existing house rule against self-labelling copy.

## Design Principles

- Show the mechanism, not just the mission: explain *how* change happens (inputs → activities → outcomes), not only that it happens.
- Evidence over sentiment: every impact claim is traceable to a source or a program page, never asserted as an unqualified virtue statement.
- Dignity over pity: girls served are shown as agents building a future, not as subjects of rescue.
- Donor-grade rigor: registration, accountability, and financials are easy to find, never buried or oversold.
- Practice restraint: warm, hopeful tone without melodrama; credibility comes from clarity and evidence, not intensity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit WCAG level stated; treat as WCAG 2.1 AA (standard for public-facing NGO/donor sites). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Body text must meet 4.5:1 contrast; this codebase already leans on `text-gray-900` / `text-gray-600` on white, which should be verified per new component rather than assumed.
