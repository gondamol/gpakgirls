Anything marked "consent needed" stays out of published pages until we have
written consent from the person, or from a parent or guardian where she was a
minor at the time. Where consent cannot be traced, crop, blur, or leave the
photo in the archive.

Original file names were sequential camera names; they were renamed for the web
and the folder was renamed from "old photos" so paths carry no spaces.
